# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-30

The national drought situation this week shows a distribution across the drought classes, with 52.6% of the area in the Normal class and 47.4% in the D0+ class. The total area affected by drought, specifically D0+ conditions, decreased by 3.8 percentage points week-on-week, moving from 51.2% in the previous week to 47.4% this week. The most severely affected states by drought area include Goa at 100.0%, Arunanchal Pradesh at 87.8%, Assam at 84.9%, Mizoram at 83.8%, Maharashtra at 82.4%, and Sikkim at 81.8%. Conversely, Odisha was the least affected state with 1.6% in drought, while Chhattisgarh had 23.2% in drought.
