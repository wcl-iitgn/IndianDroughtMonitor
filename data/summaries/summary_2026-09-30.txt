# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-30

The national drought situation this week shows a distribution across the drought classes. The cumulative area for Normal conditions was 57.5%, while the area experiencing D0 or worse conditions was 42.5%. The total drought area, specifically D0+, was 42.5% this week, which represents a contraction of 3.1 percentage points compared to the previous week's 45.6%.

The states most affected by drought were Goa with 91.1%, Jammu & Kashmir with 80.4%, and Arunanchal Pradesh with 79.4%. Other states showing significant drought impact included Karnataka with 68.2%, Bihar with 66.8%, and Maharashtra with 65.6%. Conversely, Nagaland recorded 0.7% of the area in drought, and Odisha recorded 1.3%.
