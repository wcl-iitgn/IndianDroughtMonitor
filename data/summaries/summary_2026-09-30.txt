# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-30

The national drought situation this week is characterized by a significant proportion of the country remaining in normal conditions, with a substantial area experiencing drought. The total area in the D0+ drought class was 45.1% this week. This represents a contraction in the drought area, as the percentage decreased by 6.0 points from the previous week's 51.2%. The most affected states by area in any drought were Goa at 91.1%, Jammu & Kashmir at 85.1%, Arunanchal Pradesh at 75.9%, Karnataka at 75.3%, Maharashtra at 70.3%, and Punjab at 65.7%. Conversely, Nagaland recorded 0.0% in drought, and Odisha recorded 1.3% in drought.
