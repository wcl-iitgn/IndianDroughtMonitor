# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-30

The national drought situation this week shows a significant distribution across the drought classes. The cumulative area categorized as Normal was 52.6%, while the combined area experiencing drought conditions (D0+ and above) was 70.6% (47.4% for D0+ and 27.9% for D1+). The total area experiencing drought, specifically D0+ conditions, was 47.4% this week. This represents a contraction in the drought area, as the D0+ area decreased by 3.8 percentage points week-on-week, moving from 51.2% in the previous week to 47.4% this week. The most severely affected states, based on the percentage of their area in any drought class, include Goa at 100.0%, followed by Arunanchal Pradesh at 87.8%, Assam at 84.9%, Mizoram at 83.8%, Maharashtra at 82.4%, and Sikkim at 81.8%. Conversely, Odisha was the least affected state with 1.6% in drought.
