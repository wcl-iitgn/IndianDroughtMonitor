# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-08-26

The national drought situation this week shows a distribution across the six drought classes. The cumulative area for Normal conditions was 60.2%, while the area experiencing D0 or worse drought was 39.8%. The total drought area, defined by D0 or worse conditions, was 39.8% this week, which represents an expansion of 1.0 percentage point compared to the previous week's 38.9%.

The states most affected by drought conditions were Goa, with 94.6% of its area in drought, followed by Arunachal Pradesh at 88.8%, Bihar at 88.5%, Sikkim at 86.9%, Andhra Pradesh at 74.9%, and Assam at 74.5%. Conversely, the least affected states were Odisha with 6.0%, Nagaland with 10.9%, Rajasthan with 12.5%, and Himachal Pradesh with 15.2%.

Regional conditions this week:
- North: Moderate, scattered drought. About 39% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- Northwest: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 18% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
- Northeast: Extensive drought. About 74% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- East: Widespread drought. About 44% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).

- West: Widespread drought. About 55% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- South: Widespread drought. About 49% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
