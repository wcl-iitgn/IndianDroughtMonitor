# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-11-18

About 45.5% of India's area is in some level of drought (D0 or worse), with 54.5% at normal conditions. Severe-to-extreme drought (D2 or worse) covers roughly 8.7% of the country. Compared with the previous week, the drought-affected area contracted. Conditions reflect the balance of pre-monsoon rainfall, soil moisture and runoff captured in the Combined Drought Index, with the most persistent stress across rain-fed parts of peninsular and central India.

Regional conditions this week:
- North: Moderate, scattered drought. About 39% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Northwest: Moderate, scattered drought. About 39% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Northeast: Extensive drought. About 75% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- East: Moderate, scattered drought. About 27% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Central: Moderate, scattered drought. About 27% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- West: Extensive drought. About 79% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- South: Widespread drought. About 56% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
