# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-11-18

The national drought situation this week shows a significant proportion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area in the Normal class was 54.5%, while the area experiencing drought conditions (D0 and above) was 45.5%. This week-on-week, the total drought area (D0+) contracted by 1.1 percentage points, moving from 46.6% in the previous week to 45.5%. The most affected states by area in any drought class were Goa with 100.0%, Manipur with 91.6%, Ladakh with 90.1%, Assam with 89.9%, Arunachal Pradesh with 85.0%, and Maharashtra with 82.8%. Conversely, the NCT of Delhi, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu, and Puducherry reported 0.0% of their area in drought. Odisha was the least affected state with 0.4% of its area in drought.
