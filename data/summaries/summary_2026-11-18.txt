# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-11-18

The national drought situation for the week ending 2026-11-18 shows a distribution across the six drought classes. The cumulative area categorized as Normal was 54.5%, while the combined area experiencing drought conditions (D0 and above) was 45.5%. The total area in the D0+ drought class was 45.5% this week, which represents a contraction of 1.1 percentage points compared to the previous week's 46.6%.

The most affected regions by drought status include Goa, Manipur, Ladakh, Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, and Maharashtra, with these states showing high percentages of area affected by drought. Conversely, the NCT of Delhi, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu, and Puducherry reported 0.0% of their area in drought. Odisha was the least affected state, reporting 0.4% in drought.
