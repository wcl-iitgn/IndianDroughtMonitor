# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-02

The national drought situation this week shows a significant expansion of dry conditions across the country. The cumulative area experiencing drought has increased to 46.0% in the D0+ category this week, representing a week-on-week expansion of 6.2 percentage points compared to the previous week. The distribution across drought classes is as follows: Normal at 54.0%, D0+ at 46.0%, D1+ at 25.9%, D2+ at 11.3%, D3+ at 2.0%, and D4 at 0.1%. The states most affected by drought include Goa at 94.6%, Sikkim at 86.9%, Arunachal Pradesh at 84.2%, Andhra Pradesh at 83.5%, Bihar at 79.2%, and Maharashtra at 77.0%. Conversely, Odisha, Nagaland, Himachal Pradesh, and Tripura were the least affected regions, with drought coverage at 10.2%, 10.9%, 12.8%, and 13.7% respectively.
