# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-02

The national drought situation this week is characterized by a significant expansion of the dry conditions across the country. The cumulative area under the D0+ drought class is 46.0%. This represents an expansion of 6.2 percentage points week-on-week, compared to 39.8% in the previous week and 34.3% one month ago. The distribution of drought severity shows that 25.9% of the area is in the D1+ class, and 11.3% is in the D2+ class. The most affected states by drought area include Goa with 94.6%, Sikkim with 86.9%, Arunanchal Pradesh with 84.2%, Andhra Pradesh with 83.5%, Bihar with 79.2%, and Maharashtra with 77.0%. Conversely, Odisha, Nagaland, Himachal Pradesh, and Tripura were the least affected states, with drought areas recorded at 10.2%, 10.9%, 12.8%, and 13.7% respectively.
