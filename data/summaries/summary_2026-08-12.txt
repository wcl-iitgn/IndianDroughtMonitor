# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-08-12

The national drought situation this week shows a significant proportion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area classified as Normal was 64.3%, while the combined area in drought classes D0+ through D4 was 35.7% plus 18.2% plus 6.3% plus 1.3% plus 0.0%, totaling 61.3% of the nation. The total drought area, specifically D0+ conditions, reached 35.7% this week, representing an expansion of 1.4 percentage points compared to the previous week's 34.3%.

The states most affected by drought conditions were Goa with 91.1%, Sikkim with 86.9%, and Arunachal Pradesh with 82.3%. Other states showing significant drought impact included Bihar at 71.0%, Andhra Pradesh at 70.1%, and Uttar Pradesh at 66.2%. Conversely, Gujarat, Nagaland, Odisha, and Rajasthan were among the least affected states, with percentages of 3.9%, 5.6%, 7.6%, and 11.0% respectively.
