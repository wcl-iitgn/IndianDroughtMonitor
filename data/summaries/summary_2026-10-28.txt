# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-10-28

The national drought situation this week shows a significant proportion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area categorized as Normal was 50.4%, while the area experiencing D0 or worse drought was 49.6%. This week-on-week, the total drought area, specifically D0+, increased by 2.9 percentage points compared to the previous week, moving from 46.7% to 49.6%. The most affected regions by drought presence include Goa at 92.2%, Manipur at 91.6%, Ladakh at 89.2%, Arunachal Pradesh at 84.9%, Assam at 82.7%, and Maharashtra at 82.4%. Conversely, the NCT of Delhi, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu, and Puducherry reported zero percent of their area in drought.

Regional conditions this week:
- North: Widespread drought. About 43% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Northwest: Moderate, scattered drought. About 37% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
- Northeast: Extensive drought. About 74% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- East: Moderate, scattered drought. About 38% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- Central: Moderate, scattered drought. About 33% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- West: Extensive drought. About 81% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- South: Widespread drought. About 60% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
