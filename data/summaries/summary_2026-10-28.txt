# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-10-28

The national drought situation this week shows a significant proportion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area in the Normal class is 50.4%, while the D0+ category accounts for 49.6% of the nation. The total drought area, specifically D0+, increased to 49.6% this week compared to 46.7% in the previous week, indicating an expansion of drought conditions by 2.9 percentage points week-on-week. The most affected states by area in any drought classification include Goa with 94.6%, Manipur with 93.3%, Sikkim with 85.4%, Arunachal Pradesh with 85.1%, Maharashtra with 83.1%, and Assam with 83.1%. Conversely, the NCT of Delhi recorded 0.0% in drought, and Odisha, Uttarakhand, and Nagaland were among the least affected states with percentages of 5.7%, 5.9%, and 9.9% respectively.
