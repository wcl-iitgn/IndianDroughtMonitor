# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-08-19

The national drought situation this week shows a distribution across the drought classes. The total area experiencing drought conditions, specifically D0 and above, was 35.4% of India. This figure reflects a contraction in the drought area compared to the previous week, which was 35.7%, indicating a decrease of 0.3 percentage points week-on-week. The cumulative distribution across the drought classes is as follows: Normal at 64.6%, D0+ at 35.4%, D1+ at 16.6%, D2+ at 5.5%, D3+ at 0.7%, and D4 at 0.1%. The states most affected by drought conditions were Goa with 88.4%, Tripura with 85.3%, Mizoram with 78.6%, Andhra Pradesh with 74.5%, Uttar Pradesh with 72.7%, and Telangana with 50.6%. Conversely, Rajasthan recorded 0.0% of the area in drought, while Gujarat, Odisha, and Tamil Nadu had drought areas of 4.0%, 4.8%, and 23.4% respectively.

Regional conditions this week:
- North: Extensive drought. About 100% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
- Northwest: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 8% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
- Northeast: Extensive drought. About 78% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- East: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 16% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Central: Moderate, scattered drought. About 30% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- West: Widespread drought. About 48% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- South: Widespread drought. About 45% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
