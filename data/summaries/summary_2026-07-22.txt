# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-22

The national drought situation this week is characterized by a significant proportion of the area remaining in the Normal category, with a substantial portion in the D0+ category. The total drought area, specifically D0+, was 35.9% this week, showing an expansion of 0.4 percentage points compared to the previous week, as the area was 35.5% last week. Looking further back, the area was 37.1% approximately one month ago. The states most affected by drought were Manipur with 75.4%, Arunachal Pradesh with 72.0%, Jharkhand with 68.7%, Goa with 67.9%, the NCT of Delhi with 65.2%, and Assam with 60.4%. Conversely, the least affected states were Nagaland with 5.6%, Tripura with 8.7%, Odisha with 11.7%, and Uttarakhand with 13.2%.
