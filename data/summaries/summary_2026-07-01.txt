# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-01

The national drought situation this week shows a significant area experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area in the Normal class was 58.8%, while the D0+ class accounted for 41.2% of the nation. The drought area, specifically D0+, increased by 4.1 percentage points week-on-week, rising to 41.2% compared to 37.1% in the previous week. The most severely affected regions include Goa with 100.0% of its area in drought, followed by Manipur at 98.1%, Chhattisgarh at 86.7%, Jharkhand at 73.2%, Meghalaya at 71.4%, and the NCT of Delhi at 69.6%. Conversely, Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan, Andhra Pradesh, and Karnataka were the least affected states, with their drought areas recorded at 5.0%, 6.5%, 14.8%, and 18.0% respectively.
