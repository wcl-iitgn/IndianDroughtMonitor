# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-09

The national drought situation this week shows a significant expansion of dry conditions across the country. The total area experiencing drought, categorized as D0 or worse, reached 53.2% of India. This represents an expansion of 7.3 percentage points compared to the previous week, with the area in D0+ increasing from 46.0% last week to 53.2%. The distribution of drought classes is as follows: Normal at 46.8%, D0+ at 53.2%, D1+ at 33.4%, D2+ at 15.4%, D3+ at 3.8%, and D4 at 0.1%. The states most affected by drought include Goa at 94.6%, Andhra Pradesh at 90.0%, Telangana at 86.2%, Arunachal Pradesh at 86.2%, Maharashtra at 85.2%, and Sikkim at 81.8%. Conversely, Nagaland, Tripura, Odisha, and Uttarakhand were the least affected regions with drought percentages of 5.6%, 13.7%, 19.0%, and 20.7%, respectively.
