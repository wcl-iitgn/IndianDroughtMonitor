# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-10-07

The national drought situation this week shows a significant proportion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative distribution of drought classes is as follows: Normal at 54.6%, D0+ at 45.4%, D1+ at 19.9%, D2+ at 5.9%, D3+ at 1.7%, and D4 at 0.8%. The total drought area, specifically D0+ conditions, increased to 45.4% this week, representing a week-on-week expansion of 2.9 percentage points compared to the previous week's 42.5%. The most affected states by drought area include Goa at 94.6%, Jammu & Kashmir at 82.0%, Karnataka at 76.3%, Arunachal Pradesh at 76.1%, Maharashtra at 70.4%, and Bihar at 70.0%. Conversely, Odisha was the least affected state with 1.3% in drought, while Tripura had 14.0% in drought.
