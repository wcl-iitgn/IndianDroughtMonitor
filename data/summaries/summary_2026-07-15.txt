# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-15

The national drought situation for this week shows a significant expansion of dry conditions across the country. The cumulative drought area is distributed as follows: Normal at 64.5%, D0+ at 35.5%, D1+ at 16.1%, D2+ at 6.0%, D3+ at 1.1%, and D4 at 0.0%. The total area experiencing drought conditions, specifically D0 or worse, reached 35.5%, which represents an expansion of 4.8 percentage points compared to the previous week's 30.7%.

The states most affected by drought conditions are Goa with 96.4%, Manipur with 92.9%, Jharkhand with 83.1%, Assam with 71.2%, Arunachal Pradesh with 68.1%, and the NCT of Delhi with 65.2%. Conversely, Tripura reported 0.0% in drought, while Tamil Nadu, Uttarakhand, and Rajasthan were the least affected states with drought percentages of 8.0%, 14.9%, and 17.2%, respectively.

Regional conditions this week:
- North: Widespread drought. About 59% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- Northwest: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 18% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
- Northeast: Widespread drought. About 51% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- East: Moderate, scattered drought. About 32% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Central: Moderate, scattered drought. About 36% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- West: Moderate, scattered drought. About 33% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- South: Moderate, scattered drought. About 21% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
