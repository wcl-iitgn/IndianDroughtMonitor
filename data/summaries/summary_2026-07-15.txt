# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-15

The national drought situation this week shows a significant portion of the country remains in normal conditions, with 64.5% of the area categorized as Normal. The total area experiencing drought conditions, specifically D0 or worse, was 35.5% this week. This represents an expansion of the drought area by 4.8 percentage points compared to the previous week, as the total drought area was 35.5% this week compared to 30.7% last week. The most affected regions by drought presence include Goa with 96.4%, Manipur with 92.9%, Jharkhand with 83.1%, Assam with 71.2%, Arunanchal Pradesh with 68.1%, and the NCT of Delhi with 65.2%. Conversely, Tripura had 0.0% of the area in drought, and Tamil Nadu, Uttarakhand, and Rajasthan were the least affected states with percentages of 8.0%, 14.9%, and 17.2% in drought, respectively.
