# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-10-21

The national drought situation this week shows a significant proportion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area under the Normal class is 53.3%, while the area in the D0+ class is 46.7%. This week, the total drought area, specifically D0+, was 46.7%, which represents an expansion of 0.8 percentage points compared to the previous week's 45.9%.

The states most affected by drought conditions this week include Goa with 94.6%, Manipur with 89.3%, Arunanchal Pradesh with 84.4%, Assam with 82.6%, Karnataka with 82.1%, and Jammu & Kashmir with 77.1%. Conversely, Odisha was the least affected state with 1.7% in drought, followed by Uttarakhand at 3.1%, Himachal Pradesh at 16.3%, and Nagaland at 16.6%.
