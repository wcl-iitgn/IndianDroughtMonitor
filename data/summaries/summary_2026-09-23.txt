# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-23

The national drought situation this week shows a significant distribution across the drought classes. The cumulative area in the Normal class was 48.8%, while the area experiencing D0 or worse conditions was 51.2%. The total drought area, measured by D0 or worse, was 51.2% this week, which represents a contraction of 1.4 percentage points compared to the previous week's 52.5%. Looking at the most affected states, Goa reported 100.0% of its area in drought, and the NCT of Delhi reported 95.7% in drought. Maharashtra was affected in 84.1% of its area, and Sikkim in 81.8% of its area. Conversely, the least affected states included Nagaland at 5.6%, Odisha at 10.0%, Tripura at 14.9%, and Tamil Nadu at 23.8%.
