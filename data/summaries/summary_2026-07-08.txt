# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-08

The national drought situation this week shows that the majority of India remains in a normal condition, with 69.3% of the area in the Normal class. The total area experiencing drought conditions, specifically D0 and above, was 30.7% this week. This figure reflects a contraction in the drought area, as it decreased by 10.5 percentage points compared to last week, moving from 41.2% previously. The area in the D1+ class was 15.0%, and the D2+ class accounted for 5.9%. The most affected regions by the percentage of area in any drought class included Manipur at 98.1%, Jharkhand at 78.2%, Meghalaya at 77.6%, Assam at 74.5%, Arunachal Pradesh at 70.0%, and the NCT of Delhi at 69.6%. Conversely, the least affected states were Tripura at 4.3%, Rajasthan at 6.3%, Tamil Nadu at 6.8%, and Gujarat at 10.4%.

- West: Moderate, scattered drought. About 40% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- South: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 14% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
