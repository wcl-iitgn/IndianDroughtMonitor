# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-08

The national drought situation this week shows a distribution across the six drought classes. The cumulative area for Normal conditions was 69.3%, while the combined area for D0 and above was 30.7%. The total area experiencing drought, specifically D0 and above, was 30.7% this week, compared to 41.2% in the previous week, indicating a contraction of 10.5 percentage points week-on-week.

The states most affected by drought this week include Manipur with 98.1% of its area in drought, followed by Jharkhand at 78.2%, Meghalaya at 77.6%, Assam at 74.5%, Arunanchal Pradesh at 70.0%, and the NCT of Delhi at 69.6%. Conversely, Tripura had the least affected area at 4.3%, while Gujarat had 10.4% in drought.

Regional conditions this week:
- North: Widespread drought. About 48% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- Northwest: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 10% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Northeast: Widespread drought. About 64% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- East: Widespread drought. About 44% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- Central: Moderate, scattered drought. About 24% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- West: Moderate, scattered drought. About 40% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- South: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 14% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
