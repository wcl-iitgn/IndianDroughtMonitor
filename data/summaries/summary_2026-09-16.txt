# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-09-16

The national drought situation this week shows a significant proportion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area classified as Normal is 47.5%, while the combined area in drought classes D0 and above is 52.5%.

There has been a contraction in the total drought area, with the D0+ category decreasing from 53.2% in the previous week to 52.5% this week, representing a decrease of 0.7 percentage points week-on-week. Looking further back, the area experiencing drought was 38.9% approximately one month ago.

The states most affected by drought include Goa with 94.6%, Andhra Pradesh with 92.8%, Maharashtra with 83.5%, Arunachal Pradesh with 83.3%, Sikkim with 81.8%, and Punjab with 76.0%. Conversely, the least affected states were Nagaland with 5.6%, Tripura with 13.7%, Odisha with 16.8%, and Uttarakhand with 20.4%.
