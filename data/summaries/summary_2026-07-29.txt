# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-29

The national drought situation this week shows that a significant portion of the country remains in normal conditions, with 64.9% of the area in the Normal class. The total area experiencing drought conditions, specifically D0 or worse, was 35.1% this week. This represents a contraction in the drought area compared to the previous week, as the D0+ area decreased from 35.9% last week to 35.1%, a change of -0.8 percentage points.

The states most affected by drought conditions are Goa, with 91.1% of its area in drought, followed by Sikkim at 81.8% and Arunachal Pradesh at 81.5%. Other states showing significant drought impact include Assam at 72.2%, Andhra Pradesh at 65.4%, Manipur at 64.0%, and Nagaland at 5.6%. Conversely, Rajasthan, Odisha, and Tripura were among the least affected states, with drought presence recorded at 7.5%, 7.8%, and 8.7% respectively.

Regional conditions this week:
- North: Moderate, scattered drought. About 32% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Northwest: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 12% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
- Northeast: Widespread drought. About 62% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- East: Moderate, scattered drought. About 34% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- Central: Moderate, scattered drought. About 25% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- West: Widespread drought. About 51% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- South: Widespread drought. About 41% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
