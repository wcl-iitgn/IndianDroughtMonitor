# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-11-11

The national drought situation this week is characterized by a significant portion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area classified under D0 and above is 46.6 percent. This represents a contraction in the total drought area compared to the previous week, as the percentage of area in D0+ decreased by 0.7 percentage points week-on-week. The distribution of drought severity shows that Goa, Manipur, Assam, Ladakh, Arunachal Pradesh, and Maharashtra are the most affected regions, with their areas in drought accounting for percentages of 98.7%, 91.6%, 89.1%, 88.3%, 86.6%, and 82.8 percent, respectively. Conversely, the NCT of Delhi, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu, Puducherry, and Odisha reported the least impact, with their drought area percentages at 0.0%, 0.0%, 0.0%, and 0.4 percent.

Regional conditions this week:
- North: Widespread drought. About 42% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Northwest: Widespread drought. About 40% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
- Northeast: Extensive drought. About 77% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- East: Moderate, scattered drought. About 32% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- Central: Moderate, scattered drought. About 28% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). Pockets of severe-or-worse conditions are present.
- West: Extensive drought. About 79% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
- South: Widespread drought. About 54% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).
