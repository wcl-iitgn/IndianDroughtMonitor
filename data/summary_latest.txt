# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-08

The national drought situation this week shows a majority of the area in a Normal condition, with 69.3% of India in the Normal class. The total area experiencing drought conditions, specifically D0 and above, was 30.7% this week. This figure reflects a contraction in the drought area, as it was 41.2% in the previous week, a decrease of 10.5 percentage points week-on-week. The most affected regions by drought were Manipur, with 98.1% of its area in drought, followed by Jharkhand at 78.2%, Meghalaya at 77.6%, Assam at 74.5%, Arunanchal Pradesh at 70.0%, and the NCT of Delhi at 69.6%. Conversely, the least affected states were Tripura at 4.3%, Rajasthan at 6.3%, Tamil Nadu at 6.8%, and Gujarat at 10.4%.
