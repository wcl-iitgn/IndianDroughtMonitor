# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-11-18

The national drought situation this week shows a significant portion of the country experiencing drought conditions. The cumulative area in the Normal class is 52.8%, while the combined area in D0 and above is 47.2%.

There has been an expansion in the drought area this week, with the D0+ category showing a week-on-week increase of 0.6 percentage points, as the area was 47.2% this week compared to 46.6% last week.

The most affected states by area in any drought condition include Goa at 100.0%, Manipur at 91.6%, Ladakh at 87.5%, Assam at 85.5%, Karnataka at 84.5%, and Arunachal Pradesh at 84.1%. Conversely, the least affected states are the NCT of Delhi and Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu, both at 0.0%, followed by Odisha at 0.4%, and Uttarakhand at 4.6%.
