# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-08

The national drought situation this week is characterized by a significant proportion of the country remaining in normal conditions, with a notable portion experiencing drought stress. The total area under the D0+ drought class was 30.7% this week. This figure shows a contraction trend compared to previous periods, as the area under D0+ decreased by 10.5 percentage points week-on-week, moving from 41.2% in the last week to 30.7% currently. The states most affected by drought were Manipur at 98.1%, Jharkhand at 78.2%, Meghalaya at 77.6%, Assam at 74.5%, Arunanchal Pradesh at 70.0%, and the NCT of Delhi at 69.6%. Conversely, the least affected states were Tripura at 4.3%, Rajasthan at 6.3%, Tamil Nadu at 6.8%, and Gujarat at 10.4%.
