# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-01

The national drought situation this week shows a significant expansion of dry conditions across the country. The cumulative area experiencing drought has increased to 41.2% in the D0+ category. This represents an expansion of 4.1 percentage points compared to the previous week, with the D0+ area being 41.2%, compared to 37.1% in the last week and 19.6% one month ago. The most affected regions by drought presence include Goa with 100.0%, Manipur with 98.1%, Chhattisgarh with 86.7%, Jharkhand with 73.2%, Meghalaya with 71.4%, and the NCT of Delhi with 69.6%. Conversely, the least affected states are Tamil Nadu with 5.0%, Rajasthan with 6.5%, Andhra Pradesh with 14.8%, and Karnataka with 18.0%.
