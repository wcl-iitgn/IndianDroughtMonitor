# India Drought Monitor — National Summary for week ending 2026-07-08

The national drought situation this week is characterized by a large proportion of the area remaining in the Normal category, with a significant portion in D0+ and D1+ classes. The total area experiencing drought, specifically D0+ conditions, showed a contraction this week, decreasing to 30.7% compared to 41.2% in the previous week, representing a decrease of 10.5 percentage points week-on-week. The cumulative distribution across the drought classes shows 69.3% in Normal, 30.7% in D0+, 15.0% in D1+, 5.9% in D2+, and 0.9% in D3+. The most affected regions by area in any drought class include Manipur with 98.1%, Jharkhand with 78.2%, Meghalaya with 77.6%, Assam with 74.5%, Arunachal Pradesh with 70.0%, and the NCT of Delhi with 69.6%. Conversely, Tripura was the least affected state with 4.3% in drought.

- East: Widespread drought. About 44% of the area is in drought (D0–D4). A notable share is in severe-or-worse categories (D2–D4).

- South: Mostly normal, with localised dryness. About 14% of the area is in drought (D0–D4).
